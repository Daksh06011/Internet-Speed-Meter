# Privacy Policy — Net Speed Test

_Last updated: 26 September 2026_

Net Speed Test ("the app") measures your internet connection and shows information about your device. It is built to collect as little as possible.

## What the developer collects
**Nothing.** The app has no accounts, advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or tracking of any kind. The developer does not receive any data from the app.

## What happens during a speed test
When **you** tap **Start test**, the app exchanges test data with Cloudflare's speed-test service (`speed.cloudflare.com`), using the nearest Cloudflare data centre:
- Download and upload payloads are random test bytes. They are discarded right away and are not stored.
- Like any internet connection, the test reveals your **IP address** to Cloudflare. Cloudflare returns your IP address, your internet provider's name and your approximate city to the app, so the app can show them to you.
- Cloudflare handles this traffic under its own privacy policy: https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/

No test runs automatically or in the background.

## Data stored on your device
- **Test history.** For each result the app saves the date and time, the speeds, ping and jitter, the connection type, the server, and the internet provider and IP address shown in the result. This is kept in the app's private storage on your phone only. You can delete single results or clear all history in the app, and uninstalling removes everything.
- **Settings.** Your theme, speed unit and similar choices.

## Device information
Battery, charging, temperature, memory and network details are read from Android on your device while the relevant screen is open. They are shown to you and **never leave your device**.

## Permissions
- **Internet**: to run speed tests.
- **Network state**: to show your connection type and to detect network changes during a test.

The app does not request location, phone, contacts, storage, camera or microphone access.

## Children
The app is a general-audience utility and is not directed at children. It collects no personal data from anyone.
